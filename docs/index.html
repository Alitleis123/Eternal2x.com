Upscale and smooth anime in Resolve.

Anime holds each drawing for two or three frames, so it moves in steps, and most of it reaches you as compressed 1080p. Upflow2x draws the frames in between and redraws every frame at 2x, right inside DaVinci Resolve, so it plays smooth and stays sharp on a 4K timeline.

Download, free How to install

How it works

Open the panel from Workspace, Scripts and put the playhead on a clip. This is the path it takes, and the film shows all of it in 21 seconds.

  1. Input Your clip Anything on the timeline, on 2s or 3s
  2. Analyse Marks the real drawings Puts a marker on each one. Move or add markers to choose
  3. Render Draws every in-between, then 2x RIFE 4.25 and Real-ESRGAN on your own GPU. Resolve stays usable
  4. Output Lands on the track above At a third of the speed, every frame drawn, ready to time remap
